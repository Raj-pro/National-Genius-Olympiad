About the Olympiad

The National Genius Olympiad (NGO) is a national-level competition designed to recognize young minds and empower future leaders. Built around the philosophy of Discover · Develop · Excel, it gives students a genuine platform to test what they know and how they think.

The Olympiad is designed to go beyond rote memorisation — testing conceptual understanding, logical thinking and problem-solving skills through application and concept-based questions prepared by a panel of subject-matter educators.

Open to students of Classes 5 to 12 from all recognized schools across India, in Mathematics and Science, with a certificate for every participant and a detailed performance and rank report for every candidate.

Why Participate

  • Enhance academic excellence
  • Build confidence and competitive spirit
  • Identify strengths and areas of improvement
  • Compete at national level and get recognized

Official Olympiad Syllabus

Official 89-page syllabus framework for Classes 5 to 12 in Mathematics and Science. School-aligned, concept-oriented, and designed for rigorous competitive problem-solving.

Classes 5 – 6 Group 1

Foundational Mathematics & General Science. Number sense, basic geometry, living world, matter, and logical reasoning.

Classes 7 – 8 Group 2

Intermediate algebra, geometry, ratios, integers, physics foundations, chemical changes, cells & environmental science.

Classes 9 – 10 Group 3

Advanced mathematics, coordinate geometry, trigonometry, motion, gravitation, chemical reactions, life processes & genetics.

Classes 11 – 12 Group 4

Senior calculus, vectors, electrostatics, thermodynamics, organic chemistry, modern biology & higher problem solving.

Eligibility & Class Groups

Who Can Participate

Students from Classes 5 to 12, studying in any recognized school across India, are eligible. Students may register individually or through their school's Olympiad coordinator. There is no minimum academic requirement.

Each subject paper is a separate 60-minute, multiple-choice test comprising a curriculum section, a logical reasoning section, and an achievers' section. Exact question count, section-wise weightage and marking scheme for each class group will be published in the official information brochure before registrations open.

MCQ Based Exam
